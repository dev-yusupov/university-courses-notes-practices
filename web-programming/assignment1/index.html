Budapest Metro Builder

Plan the metro network of Budapest!

Game Rules

Objective

Build Budapest's metro network to earn the most points across 4 rounds.

How to Play

  • 4 Rounds: Build one metro line per round (M1/Yellow, M2/Red, M3/Blue, M4/Green) in random order.
  • Draw Cards: Each turn, draw a station card (A, B, C, D, or Joker).
  • Build Segments: Click on a station to extend your metro line from an endpoint.
  • Rules:
    • Segments must be straight (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal at 45°)
    • Must match the card type or connect to Deák tér (Joker station)
    • Cannot cross other segments or pass through stations
    • Cannot revisit stations on the same line
  • Round Ends: After drawing 8 cards.
  • Scoring: Points for districts covered, Danube crossings, railway stations, and junctions.

Controls

Drawing Segments: Click on a target station after drawing a card to extend your metro line.

Skip: Click "Draw Next Card" to skip building a segment.
